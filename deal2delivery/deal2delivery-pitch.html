Every week, four things go wrong.
Nobody sees any of them coming.
Salesforce knows the customers. SAP knows the warehouse. The two systems have never talked.
📦
Monday
The GPU reorder never fires.
80 gaming GPU orders land in Salesforce over the weekend. SAP sees nothing. Stock hits zero by noon. The reorder fires three days too late.
🔕
Tuesday
A loyal customer quietly leaves.
Three warranty cases on a premium TV — escalating in SAP for six weeks. Nobody in Salesforce knew. The customer churns and the account is marked lost.
📊
Wednesday
Christmas stock ordered on gut feel.
Procurement picks holiday laptop quantities from memory — no lag features, no ML. 400 units of the wrong model arrive in November. Dead stock until March.
🤖
Thursday
"Why is this customer High Risk?"
The model flagged them. The manager wants to act. Nobody can open the black box. No intervention happens. The at-risk account is lost.
